What you receive

A PDF ebook (75 pages, A4). You need a PDF reader; nothing else is required.

How and when

Delivery starts only after your payment has been confirmed by our payment provider. We then email a secure link to the address you used at checkout. It opens a download page on this site, where you press a button to download the PDF. Delayed payment methods can take longer to confirm; your link is sent once they do.

Download links

Links are time-limited and allow a limited number of downloads. The exact limits are stated in your email. If a link expires or you lose it, request a new one from your order page or by contacting support; a new link replaces the old one.

If something goes wrong

Check your spam folder first. If the email still has not arrived, use the resend form on your order page, or email with your order reference and we will fix it.

Your order page

The email also links to an order page showing the status of your order (for example paid, refunded). Keep your order reference for support requests.
