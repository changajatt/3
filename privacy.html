What we collect

Depending on the payment provider you use, that provider may act as the merchant of record (the legal seller of your purchase). If so, its own terms and privacy policy also apply to your payment, and it may share limited order details with us (such as your email, an order reference and the payment status). This website does not set cookies of its own and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. When you buy, collects your payment details, name and email on its own checkout page. We never see or store your card number or security code.

Our order system receives and stores only what is needed to fulfil and support your order: your email address, an order reference, the payment status, the amount and currency, timestamps, and whether the download email was sent. We also keep a one-way hash of your email address to find your order if you ask for a new download link, a count of downloads for each link, and status changes such as refunds or payment disputes reported by the payment provider. Download links are stored only as one-way hashes.

To limit abuse we keep short-lived request counters tied to a one-way hash of your IP address; they are deleted automatically within about two days. We do not store your raw IP address in our order records.

How we use it

  • To confirm payment, deliver your purchase and send your download link.
  • To resend a link and answer support requests.
  • To prevent duplicate or fraudulent orders and to meet accounting and legal duties.

How long we keep it

We keep order records only as long as needed for support, accounting and legal obligations. You can ask us to erase your order record by emailing us from the address you used at checkout; we may keep limited records where the law or our payment provider requires it.
